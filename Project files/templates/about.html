MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning

HematoVision leverages MobileNetV2 pre-trained on the ImageNet visual database. MobileNetV2 uses depthwise separable convolutions and inverted residual blocks with linear bottlenecks, rendering it computationally efficient and ideal for biomedical image classification.

  • Feature Extractor: Frozen convolutional base preserving low-level edge, texture, and cellular membrane patterns.
  • Custom Classification Head: Global Average Pooling 2D layer coupled with dense ReLU and Dropout (0.3) regularization.
  • Output Layer: Softmax activation mapping 4 distinct leukocyte probabilities.

Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)

To eliminate black-box opacity in medical decision making, HematoVision integrates Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM).

Grad-CAM uses the gradients of any target concept flowing into the final convolutional layer to produce a coarse localization map highlighting the important regions in the image for predicting the concept.

Enables hematologists to verify whether the AI focused on nuclear lobulation, cytoplasmic granules, or background artifacts.
